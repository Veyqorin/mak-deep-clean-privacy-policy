01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mak Deep Clean (Android package com.mak.deepclean) handles information when you install and use the application.

We believe in clear, accurate disclosures. Mak Deep Clean performs all storage analysis, duplicate detection, and file cleaning locally on your device, while using Google's advertising and consent SDKs to display ads and manage advertising consent preferences.

02

Information We Collect

Data collected directly by us: We do not require account registration, we do not operate our own backend servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data or personal files on our own servers.

Data automatically processed by third-party advertising SDKs: Because Mak Deep Clean integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device, network, and interaction data when ads or consent requests are loaded, subject to your consent choices and applicable laws. See Section 5 and Section 6 for details.

03

Local File Processing

Mak Deep Clean scans storage metrics, duplicate photos, videos, audio, files, and app-owned temporary cache locally on your device:

  • Your personal photos, videos, audio, documents, and filenames are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Local scan caches (such as perceptual and file hash indices stored in mak_photo_hashes.db) stay on your device only.
  • App appearance preferences (selected theme and light/dark mode) are stored locally using Android DataStore (mak_deep_clean_settings).
  • File deletions require explicit user confirmation through Android system dialogs where applicable.
04

05

Google AdMob Advertising

Mak Deep Clean uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display Banner and Interstitial advertisements.

To serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and support ad personalization (where permitted), Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device or other identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address.
  • App interactions: ad impressions, clicks, and related interaction diagnostics.
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics: diagnostic information related to the Ads SDK.

For more information on how Google processes advertising data, please review Google Privacy & Terms — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

06

Google UMP and Consent

Mak Deep Clean integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and respect user advertising consent preferences:

  • On startup, the app checks consent requirements through Google UMP. Not every user is shown a consent form; forms are presented when required for your region under applicable privacy frameworks (such as EEA, UK, or applicable US state regulations, once configured in the publisher's AdMob account).
  • Before Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized or ad requests are made, the app checks whether consent status permits requesting ads.
  • When a consent form is displayed, you can choose whether to allow personalized or non-personalized ads according to the options presented in the form.
  • Where privacy options are required, you can review or update your choices at any time inside the app via Settings → Ad Privacy Options.
Important note: Integrating Google UMP provides technical consent controls within the app, while overall regulatory compliance depends on regional legal requirements and publisher configuration in Google AdMob.
07

Data Sharing

Personal files: Mak Deep Clean does not share, sell, or transmit your personal photos, videos, audio files, documents, or folder contents to us or to any third party.

Advertising SDK data: When advertisements are requested, data collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (described in Section 5) may be shared with Google and its advertising partners to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements in accordance with your consent settings and Google's policies.

08

Internet and Network Usage

Mak Deep Clean does use internet access when available, strictly for:

  • Checking consent requirements and loading consent forms via the Google UMP SDK.
  • Requesting and displaying Banner and Interstitial advertisements via the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Core file-scanning, duplicate-detection, and local cleaning features operate on-device and do not upload your files to the internet. If your device is offline at startup, consent errors are handled gracefully without crashing and without bypassing required consent checks.

09

Data Storage and Security

  • Local database exclusions: Local scan hash caches (mak_photo_hashes.db and related journal/WAL files) are stored inside the app's private storage and explicitly excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Local preferences: Theme and appearance mode preferences are stored locally in Android DataStore.
  • Encrypted transit: Network communications performed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google UMP SDK are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Deletion safety: User media deletions require explicit confirmation through Android's system dialogs where applicable.
10

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not operate backend servers or user accounts, we do not retain your personal data on any remote servers.

  • Local app data: Local scan caches and appearance preferences remain on your device only until you clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Mak Deep Clean.
  • Advertising data: Data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained and managed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
11

Children's Privacy

Mak Deep Clean is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, please contact us using the details in Section 15 so we can assist appropriately.

12

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data and advertising preferences. You can exercise control directly on your device:

  • Manage ad consent in-app: Where privacy options are required for your region, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options inside Mak Deep Clean to review or change your consent choices.
  • Reset or delete Advertising ID: Open your device's Android Settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your Android Advertising ID.
  • Manage media permissions: Revoke or adjust storage/media permissions at any time in Android's App Info settings for Mak Deep Clean.
  • Remove local data: Clear the app's storage or uninstall Mak Deep Clean to remove all locally stored caches and preferences.
13

Third-Party Services

Mak Deep Clean integrates only the following third-party services that communicate over the network:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Google Privacy Policy · How Google Uses Partner Data
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK: Used to check consent requirements and present Google-managed consent and privacy options forms.

Mak Deep Clean does not include third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or cloud file-upload services.
