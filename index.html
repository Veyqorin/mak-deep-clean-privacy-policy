# Privacy Policy for Mak Deep Clean **Last Updated:** November 24, 2026 **Package Name:** `com.mak.deepclean` ## 1. Local File Processing Mak Deep Clean scans storage metrics, duplicate photos, videos, audio, files, and app-owned temporary cache **locally on your device**. - Your personal photos, videos, audio, documents, and filenames are **never uploaded** to our servers. - Local scan caches (such as perceptual/file hash indices) are stored only on your device and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. - App appearance preferences (theme and light/dark mode) are stored locally using Android DataStore. - File deletions require explicit user confirmation through Android system dialogs where applicable. ## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob) Mak Deep Clean uses **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display Banner and Interstitial advertisements. - To serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and support ad personalization (where permitted), Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain data, including: - Device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) - Approximate location (derived from IP address) - App interactions (ad impressions, clicks, and related diagnostics) - Crash logs and performance diagnostics related to the Ads SDK - We do not operate our own backend servers and do not collect or store your personal data ourselves. - For more information on how Google processes data, see [Google Privacy & Terms — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). ## 3. Consent & Privacy Preferences (GDPR / UK / US Privacy) Mak Deep Clean integrates the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK**: - Where required by applicable law (such as the EEA, UK, or applicable US states), the app requests your consent before loading advertisements. - You may choose whether to allow personalized or non-personalized ads according to the options presented in the consent form. - Where privacy options are required, you can review or update your choices at any time from **Settings → Ad Privacy Options** inside the app. - You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device's Android Settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** or **Privacy → Ads**). ## 4. Permissions Used - **Media / Storage Permissions** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, and `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 12 and below): Used solely on-device to find duplicate media and analyze storage usage. - **Network Permissions** (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Used exclusively by Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google UMP SDK to load consent information and display advertisements. ## 5. Children's Privacy Mak Deep Clean is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 6. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Any updates will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.
